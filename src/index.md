## Announcements

 We have six rooms of glass, including selections of Depression, Elegant, Mid-Century Modern, Carnival, EAPG and Brilliant Cut. We also have a well stocked Gift Shop, where we offer selected items that are either duplicates of our permanent collection, or items donated specifically for sale to support the Museum.

If you have an interest in a group tour, use the "Contact Us" form on our web site and we may be able to accommodate you. Please be sure to include your contact information.
