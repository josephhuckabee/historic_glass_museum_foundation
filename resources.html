Provident Bank

Participants who bank at Provident Bank, available throughout Riverside and San Bernardino counties, may choose to link their accounts to the Historical Glass Museum Foundation.

Based on the confidential linkages of depositors' accounts, Provident makes a donation to the Historical Glass Museum. The donation is funded by Provident and does not come from depositors' accounts.

In past years, these donations have amounted to as much as $500 or more. It does not cost participants anything to sign up.
