Unsubscribe from Museum emails.

You may ask the Historical Glass Museum to stop sending marketing and newsletter emails at any time.

Request Removal

The Museum's online mailing-list service is not connected yet, so this page cannot automatically remove an address.

For now, send a request from the email address you want removed. No account, password, payment, or additional personal information is required.

Email an Unsubscribe Request

Your email program will open with a prepared message. The request is not sent until you send that email.

What Happens Next

A Museum representative must remove your address from the current mailing records and keep it on the suppression list for future marketing and newsletter mailings.

When an email service is connected, this page and every applicable Museum marketing email must use that service's working unsubscribe process.
