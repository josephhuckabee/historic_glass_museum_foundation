About the Shop

  • The Gift Shop offers fine collectibles and unique gifts.
  • Items are donated for sale or are duplicates and excess items from the Museum collection.
  • The Fenton Art Glass section is popular with collectors and visitors.
  • The shop also offers used glass reference books.
  • The Gift Shop is open Saturday and Sunday, noon-4:00 PM.
