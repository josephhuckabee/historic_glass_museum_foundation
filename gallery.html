Museum Gallery

The Museum is housed in a century-old Victorian house, with cases of glass throughout the building. Display contents change from time to time to keep the experience fresh for visitors.

Photographing glass is difficult, especially inside enclosed cases. Reflections, close quarters, and case lighting can make it hard to capture the full object, so in-person viewing remains the best way to experience the collection.

Featured Areas

  • Elegant glass
  • Mid-Century Modern glass
  • Carnival glass
  • Early American Pattern Glass
  • Brilliant Cut glass
