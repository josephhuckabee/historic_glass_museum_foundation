Gift Shop

Collectibles and reference books that support the Museum.

Redlands' Hidden Secret for Gifts

The Museum Gift Shop offers collectibles and unique gifts for visitors and glass enthusiasts.

  • Items are donated for sale or are duplicates and excess items from the Museum collection.
  • The Fenton Art Glass section is popular with collectors and visitors.
  • The shop offers used glass reference books.
  • Fresh finds are added as donated inventory becomes available.
  • Occasional sale events may offer additional discounts.

Shop Hours

The Gift Shop is open during Museum hours.

Saturday and Sunday: noon to 4:00 p.m.

Gift Shop sales support Museum operations and care for the collection.
